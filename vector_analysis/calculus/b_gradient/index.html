2. Gradient for scalar

매개변수 공간 → 실공간 직교좌표계

$$ \nabla_u :=\hat{u}_j\frac{\partial}{\partial u_j} \implies \nabla :=\hat{e}_j\frac{\partial}{h_j\partial u_j} $$
  • 데카르트좌표계:
$$\hat{u}_1=\hat{u}_x, \hat{u}_2=\hat{u}_y, \hat{u}_3=\hat{u}_z$$$$h_1=1, h_2=1, h_3=1$$$$\hat{e}_1=\hat{x}, \hat{e}_2=\hat{y}, \hat{e}_3=\hat{z}$$
  • 원통좌표계:
$$\hat{u}_1=\hat{u}_\rho, \hat{u}_2=\hat{u}_\phi, \hat{u}_3=\hat{u}_z$$$$h_1=1, h_2=\rho, h_3=1$$$$\hat{e}_1=\hat{\rho}, \hat{e}_2=\hat{\phi}, \hat{e}_3=\hat{z}$$
  • 구좌표계:
$$\hat{u}_1=\hat{u}_r, \hat{u}_2=\hat{u}_\theta, \hat{u}_3=\hat{u}_\phi$$$$h_1=1, h_2=r, h_3=r\sin\theta$$$$\hat{e}_1=\hat{r}, \hat{e}_2=\hat{\theta}, \hat{e}_3=\hat{\phi}$$
