(a) Algebra structure

1. 양방향 라플라스 변환의 대수 구조

작용 순위: 연산자 → 괄호 → 켓

  • 이동 연산 1
$$ \langle s^L|e^{at}f(t)\rangle =\langle s^L|e^{a\hat{t}}|f\rangle =\langle (s-a)^L|f\rangle $$$$ \langle t|e^{as}f(s)\rangle =\langle t|e^{a\hat{s}}|f\rangle =\langle t+a|f\rangle $$
  • 이동 연산 2
$$ \langle s^L|f(t+a)\rangle =\langle s^L|e^{a\hat{s}}|f\rangle =e^{as}\langle s^L|f\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(s+a)\rangle =\langle t|e^{-a\hat{t}}|f\rangle =e^{-at}\langle t|f\rangle $$
  • scaling 1
$$ \langle s^L|f(at)\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}\left\langle\left(\frac{s}{a}\right)^L\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(as)\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}\left\langle\frac{t}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • scaling 2
$$ \langle s^L|f(at+b)\rangle =\left\langle s^L\middle|e^{\frac{b}{a}\hat{s}}\middle|f(at)\right\rangle =e^{\frac{b}{a}s}\left\langle s^L\middle|f(at)\right\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}e^{\frac{b}{a}s}\left\langle\left(\frac{s}{a}\right)^L\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(as+b)\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|e^{-\frac{b}{a}\hat{t}}\middle|f(as)\right\rangle =e^{-\frac{b}{a}t}\left\langle t\middle|f(as)\right\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}e^{-\frac{b}{a}t}\left\langle\frac{t}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 미분 1
$$ \left\langle s^L\middle|\frac{d^n}{dt^n}f(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle s^L\middle|(\hat{D}_t)^n\middle|f\right\rangle =s^n\left\langle s^L\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|\frac{d^n}{ds^n}f(s)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{D}_s)^n\middle|f\right\rangle =(-t)^n\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 미분 2
$$ \left\langle s^L\middle|t^nf(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle s^L\middle|(\hat{t})^n\middle|f\right\rangle =(-1)^n\frac{d^n}{ds^n}\left\langle s\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|s^nf(s)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{s})^n\middle|f\right\rangle =\frac{d^n}{dt^n}\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 적분 1
$$ \left\langle s^L\middle|\int_{-\infty}^{t} dt'f(t')\right\rangle =\left\langle s^L\middle|(\hat{D}_t)^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =s^{-1}\left\langle s\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|\int_{\mathbb{C}} ds'f(s')\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{D}_s)^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =-t^{-1}\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 적분 2
$$ \left\langle s^L\middle|t^{-1}f(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle s^L\middle|(\hat{t})^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =-\int_{\infty}^{s}ds'\left\langle s'\middle|f\right\rangle =\int_{s}^{\infty}ds'\left\langle s'\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|s^{-1}f(s)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{s})^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =\int_{-\infty}^{t}dt'\left\langle t'\middle|f\right\rangle $$

2. 푸리에 변환의 대수 구조

푸리에 변환/역변환의 대수 구조는, 정규/비정규 직교 기저가 동일하다.

작용 순위: ‘켓’이 가장 후순위

  • 이동 연산 1
$$ \langle \omega|e^{at}f(t)\rangle =\langle \omega|e^{a\hat{t}}|f\rangle =\langle \omega+ia|f\rangle $$$$ \langle t|e^{a\omega}f(\omega)\rangle =\langle t|e^{a\hat{\omega}}|f\rangle =\langle t-ia|f\rangle $$
  • 이동 연산 2
$$ \langle \omega|f(t+a)\rangle =\langle \omega|e^{ia\hat{\omega}}|f\rangle =e^{ia\omega}\langle \omega|f\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(\omega+a)\rangle =\langle t|e^{-ia\hat{t}}|f\rangle =e^{-iat}\langle t|f\rangle $$
  • scaling 1
$$ \langle \omega|f(at)\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}\left\langle\frac{\omega}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(a\omega)\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}\left\langle\frac{t}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • scaling 2
$$ \langle \omega|f(at+b)\rangle =\left\langle \omega\middle|e^{i\frac{b}{a}\hat{\omega}}\middle|f(at)\right\rangle =e^{i\frac{b}{a}\omega}\left\langle \omega\middle|f(at)\right\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}e^{i\frac{b}{a}\omega}\left\langle\frac{\omega}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \langle t|f(a\omega+b)\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|e^{-i\frac{b}{a}\hat{t}}\middle|f(a\omega)\right\rangle =e^{-i\frac{b}{a}t}\left\langle t\middle|f(a\omega)\right\rangle =\frac{1}{|a|}e^{-i\frac{b}{a}t}\left\langle\frac{t}{a}\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 미분 1
$$ \left\langle \omega\middle|\frac{d^n}{dt^n}f(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle \omega\middle|(\hat{D}_t)^n\middle|f\right\rangle =(i\omega)^n\left\langle \omega\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|\frac{d^n}{d\omega^n}f(\omega)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{D}_\omega)^n\middle|f\right\rangle =(-it)^n\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 미분 2
$$ \left\langle \omega\middle|t^nf(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle \omega\middle|(\hat{t})^n\middle|f\right\rangle =i^n\frac{d^n}{d\omega^n}\left\langle \omega\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|\omega^nf(\omega)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{\omega})^n\middle|f\right\rangle =(-i)^n\frac{d^n}{dt^n}\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 적분 1
$$ \left\langle \omega\middle|(\hat{D}_t)^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =\langle\omega|u\ast f\rangle =\left\lbrace\pi\delta(\omega)+\frac{1}{i\omega}\right\rbrace f(\omega) =\pi\delta(\omega)f(0)+\frac{1}{i\omega}f(\omega) $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|(\hat{D}_\omega)^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =(-it)^{-1}\left\langle t\middle|f\right\rangle $$
  • 적분 2
$$ \left\langle \omega\middle|t^{-1}f(t)\right\rangle =\left\langle \omega\middle|(\hat{t})^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =(i)^{-1}\int_{\infty}^{\omega}d\omega'\left\langle \omega'\middle|f\right\rangle =i\int_{\omega}^{\infty}d\omega'\left\langle \omega'\middle|f\right\rangle $$$$ \left\langle t\middle|\omega^{-1}f(\omega)\right\rangle =\left\langle t\middle|(\hat{\omega})^{-1}\middle|f\right\rangle =(-i)^{-1}\int_{-\infty}^{t}dt'\left\langle t'\middle|f\right\rangle =i\int_{-\infty}^{t}dt'\left\langle t'\middle|f\right\rangle $$
