(b) Phasor transform

페이저 변환을 수행할 수 있는 조건

  • 입력과 출력이 단일 주기와 단일 파장을 가진다.
  • 연산자가 self-adjoint 일 경우, 특수해는 전체해가 된다.

1. Phasor 변환

$$ A\cos\left(\omega t+\phi\right) \to Ae^{i\phi} $$$$ A\sin\left(\omega t+\phi\right)\to-iAe^{i\phi} $$$$ A\cos\left(\omega t+\phi\right) \to Ae^{i\phi} $$$$ A\sin\left(\omega t+\phi\right)\to-iAe^{i\phi} $$$$ A\cos\left(\omega t-kx+\phi\right)\to Ae^{i\left(-kx+\phi\right)} $$$$ A\sin\left(\omega t-kx+\phi\right)\to-iAe^{i\left(-kx+\phi\right)} $$$$ Ae^{ikx}e^{-i\omega t}\to Ae^{ikx} $$

$e^{-i\omega t}$를 기준으로,

$$ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\to-i\omega $$$$ \frac{\partial^2}{\partial t^2}\to-\omega^2 $$

$e^{+i\omega t}$를 기준으로,

$$ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\to i\omega $$$$ \frac{\partial^2}{\partial t^2}\to-\omega^2 $$

example1-1)

$$ y''-y'-2y=e^{ix} $$
sol
$$ \left(-1-i-2\right)\underline{y_p}=1 $$ $$ \underline{y_p}=-\frac{1}{3+i}=-\frac{3-i}{10} $$ $$ y_{p}=-\frac{3-i}{10}e^{ix} $$

example1-2)

$$ y''-y'-2y=\cos x $$
sol
$$ \left(-1-i-2\right)\underline{y_p}=1 $$ $$ \underline{y_p}=-\frac{1}{3+i}=-\frac{3-i}{10} $$ $$ y_{p}=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack-\frac{3-i}{10}e^{ix}\right\rbrack=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack-\frac{3-i}{10}\left(\cos x+i\sin x\right)\right\rbrack=-\frac{3}{10}\cos x-\frac{1}{10}\sin x $$

example2-1)

$$ y''-3y'-4y=-ie^{i2x} $$
sol
$$ (-4-6i-4)\underline{y_p}=-1i $$ $$ y_p=\frac{i}{8+6i}e^{i2x} $$

example2-2)

$$ y''-3y'-4y=\sin 2x $$
sol
$$ (-4-6i-4)\underline{y_p}=-1i $$ $$ y_p=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{i}{8+6i}e^{i2x}\right\rbrack=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{8i-6}{100}\left(\cos x+i\sin x\right)\right\rbrack=-\frac{3}{50}\cos x-\frac{2}{25}\sin x $$

example3)

$$ y''+y'-2y=4\sin 2x $$
sol
$$ (-4+i-2)\underline{y_p}=-1i $$ $$ y_p=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{-i}{-4+i-2}e^{i2x}\right\rbrack=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{6i-1}{37}\left(\cos x+i\sin x\right)\right\rbrack=-\frac{1}{37}\cos x-\frac{6}{37}\sin x $$

example4)

$$ y''+25y=32\cos 3x $$
sol
$$ (-9+25)\underline{y_p}=32 $$ $$ y_p=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{32}{16}e^{i3x}\right\rbrack=2\cos 3x $$

example5)

$$ y''+4y=\sin 2x $$
sol
$$ \left(s^2+4\right)\underline{y_p}=-1i,\quad s=2i $$ $$ \underline{y_p}=\frac{1}{s-2i}\cdot\frac{-i}{\left.\left(s+2i\right)\right|_{s=2i}}=x\cdot\frac{-i}{4i}=-\frac14x $$ $$ y_{p}=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack-\frac{x}{4}e^{i2x}\right\rbrack=-\frac{x}{4}\cos2x $$

example6)

$$ x^2y''-2xy'+2y=\cos x $$
sol
$$ \left(s^2-3s+2\right)\underline{y_p}=1 $$ $$ \underline{y_p}=\frac{1}{\left(s^2-3s+2\right)\left|_{s=i}\right.}=\frac{1}{-3i+1} $$ $$ y_{p}=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack\frac{3i+1}{10}\left(\cos x+i\sin x\right)\right\rbrack=\frac{1}{10}\cos x-\frac{3}{10}\sin x $$

example7)

$$ x^2y''+xy'+25y=3\sin3x $$
sol
$$ \left(s^2+25\right)\underline{y_p}=-3i $$ $$ \underline{y_p}=\frac{-3i}{\left(s-5i\right)\left(s+5i\right)\left|_{s=3i}\right.} =-\frac{3}{16}i $$ $$ y_{p}=\operatorname{Re}\left\lbrack-\frac{3}{16}i\left(\cos x+i\sin x\right)\right\rbrack =\frac{3}{16}\sin x $$
